# Chapter 6 Lab: Image and Video Generation Solutions

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Generate image with DALL-E via Foundry SDK: craft prompt with style and composition guidance, apply negative prompt to exclude unwanted elements, use inpainting to modify specific region with mask, verify watermark presence

## Key takeaway

Negative prompts prevent unwanted visual elements; inpainting for targeted edits (masked region only); watermarks required for generated content provenance.
